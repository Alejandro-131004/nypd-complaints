# Educational Programs

It's no use expecting minors to report their own issues if they are too scared or even unaware they are being abused. It's common for individuals stuck in recurrently abusive or toxic environments to become desensitised to situations, which can lead to the development of dangerous tedencies for perpetuating harmful actions (becoming offenders) or not recognizing their prolongued suffering is not a normal day-to-day occurrence.

Ultimately, victims may not recognise abuse as abnormal.

Including healthy interpersonal and sexual relations educational sessions will allow students to recognize already occurring problems and potentially prevent future offences from even happening. 


# Public Transportation Crimes

For crimes commited inside or around the entrances-exits of public transportation, I suggest the implementation of more regular and undercover security officers.

Uniformed officers will serve as visual layer of safety for children and teenagers, and will discourage offenders.

Undercover security keeps an eye out for potential underage victims:
 1. during trips, identifying potential harassment inside public transportation vehicles, where victims have no way of distancing themselves from offenders
 2. at the moments of entering of exiting vehicles, officers can identify suspicious behaviour (following, targeting), which is also tied to behaviour inside vehicles

## Extracurricular Activities

I propose the increase of availability in after-class extracurricular activities, where students can feel safe and allow parents to carry out work commute more efficiently without having to risk long, unsupervised periods of time at residences.

Focusing now on more critical aspects of student interaction, it's no use creating spaces and activities if students aren't encouraged to join them. 

It's no secret peer pressure and bullying will play a huge part in how children and teenagers choose to stay after-hours or simply go home. To mitigate this issue, I propose study group spaces as well as simple, dedicated clubs. This will allow all student groups to choose school resource aided productivity or simple social interaction with other people who share the same interests.

Aditionally, if possible, always having on-call, easy and anonymously accessible psychologists or dedicated professionals will increase the likelihood of students reporting their own abuse, especially if they can use excuses like "Can I stay for study group?"
